What you just saw

A useful road changed the route choices.

The connection itself stayed quick. The harm came from where it sent traffic: every shortcut journey crossed both narrow bridges, so the two old bottlenecks carried more of the morning peak.

This is conditional, not a rule against roads. With lighter traffic, the same link in the same model saves about 8 seconds.

Model & evidence

This is a synthetic network. It demonstrates a mechanism; it does not represent Canberra or any real place.

Traffic is simulated car by car. Vehicles follow the Intelligent Driver Model at a fixed 0.25-second timestep. Queues emerge behind two modelled bridge bottlenecks; no formula adds delay when the connector opens.

Route choice is shared learning. Completed trips update one running estimate for each route. Each new driver then makes a seeded, probabilistic choice; quicker-looking routes are more likely, never mandatory.

Animation time is compressed. The presentation advances many fixed 0.25-second steps between frames, pauses at exact checkpoints and never changes the physics, seed or evidence.

The verdict is a paired counterfactual. The road-closed and road-open experiments reuse the same generated departure schedule, demand and random seed. The only treatment difference is whether the connector is available. The animated waves are one warm-start illustration; their checkpoint choice shares are not used as the scientific result.

The chosen seeded result is modest: 5:31 to 5:44, or 3.8% longer. In its open-road cohort, 106 of 280 trips used the connector (38%). The result passed the longer-horizon check.

There is no 4% threshold for a paradox. Direction and experimental control matter more than a large effect. Across 8 usable runs from 10 seed attempts, the mean increase was 4.2%; two unsettled attempts were treated as inconclusive and excluded from that average.

Source. M. Treiber, A. Hennecke & D. Helbing, “Congested traffic states in empirical observations and microscopic simulations” , Physical Review E 62 (2000).
